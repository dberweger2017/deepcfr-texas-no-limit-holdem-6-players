# Repository guidance

Read [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) before starting a task. Its **How we work** section defines the branch, commit, push, PR, review, and merge workflow, along with code-comment conventions and decisions reserved for the owner.

Use **Current position** to select the next task and update it when work lands. The roadmap records direction and acceptance checks; it does not schedule unattended work. Consult [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md) before changing game behavior or player information.

Large untracked artifacts and cleanup destinations are indexed in [RESULTS_INDEX.md](RESULTS_INDEX.md). Keep working files wherever convenient; at cleanup archive them to the designated Google Drive folder with manifests, failures and retrieval provenance. The owner authorized removing local research originals once Google Drive confirms their matching archives uploaded. Apply that authorization only to merged PR evidence after current upload acceptance, archive/member hash checks and dependency review. Retain open PR roots, active inputs, shared Git, changed or unarchived files and other agents’ dependencies. Never delete inside synced folders or force offloading. Record removed paths and restoration links in a cleanup receipt and the index. This policy does not schedule unattended cleanup.

Before copying, archiving, moving or cleaning research files, check the owning PR’s current status. Leave open PR work roots and their dependencies untouched unless the owner explicitly authorizes that specific action. Finish archival work on merged runs without interfering with active research.

Research model storage: training checkpoints, inference exports, replay buffers and generated research bundles belong in the designated Research-Cloud folder. Agents retrieve them into ignored local working directories (`models/`, `results/` or `planning/`) when needed and verify their SHA256 against the index/manifest. Record the Drive URL or ID, archive member path, hash and retrieval command in RESULTS_INDEX.md. Do not force-add research binaries. A model may enter Git only as an explicitly owner-approved release artifact with an exact-path ignore exception; a release name alone does not grant approval. Existing released models remain available through their pinned release downloads.

For the shared Git/Drive boundary and required archive receipts, follow [docs/artifact-storage.md](docs/artifact-storage.md). Before committing, stage the intended files and run `python -m scripts.check_repository_artifacts`; CI rejects new research payloads and changes to grandfathered payload bytes. Do not regenerate the retention list to admit new experiment output. [docs/development.md](docs/development.md) maps supported runtime and research entry points.
