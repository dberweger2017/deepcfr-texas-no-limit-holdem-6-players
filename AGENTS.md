# Repository guidance

Read [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) before starting a task. Its **How we work** section defines the branch, commit, push, PR, review, and merge workflow, along with code-comment conventions and decisions reserved for the owner.

Use **Current position** to select the next task and update it when work lands. The roadmap records direction and acceptance checks; it does not schedule unattended work. Consult [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md) before changing game behavior or player information.

Large untracked artifacts and cleanup destinations are indexed in [RESULTS_INDEX.md](RESULTS_INDEX.md). Keep working files wherever convenient; at cleanup archive them to the designated Google Drive folder with manifests, failures and retrieval provenance. The owner authorized removing local research originals once Google Drive confirms their matching archives uploaded. Apply that authorization only to merged PR evidence after current upload acceptance and dependency review. For cleanup, trust Google Drive's confirmed upload status and use existing manifests and provenance; the owner does not require downloading archives or repeating archive/member hash audits. Check that selected originals still match their recorded paths, sizes and modification history; retain changed or uncertain files. Retain open PR roots, active inputs, shared Git, changed or unarchived files and other agents’ dependencies. Never delete inside synced folders or force offloading. Record removed paths and restoration links in a cleanup receipt and the index. The sole scheduled exception is the owner-approved October 16 bb128 cache eviction described below; this does not authorize other unattended cleanup.

Before copying, archiving, moving or cleaning research files, check the owning PR’s current status. Leave open PR work roots and their dependencies untouched unless the owner explicitly authorizes that specific action. Finish archival work on merged runs without interfering with active research.

The owner gave standing permission on October 14 to remove inactive, rebuildable caches and other regenerable output without separate approval when there is no data loss and no current active PR is affected. This includes derived policy caches and build output even when excluded from research archives. Confirm their generating inputs and rebuild method, protect active dependencies, and record removed paths plus source/input pins and regeneration commands. Unique scientific evidence, checkpoints and inference originals still follow the upload/retention rules above; being repeatable does not turn a research run into a cache. This permission does not schedule unattended cleanup.

Research model storage: Google Drive (`deepcfr-research-results`, root ID `188bEt6i0RHqegCCdvpf3wPzUiRw78N2s`) is the long-term store for all research files. New research uploads go ONLY through bb128's HDD cache using `gdrive:`. Use `/research/PR-<n>-<name>/` (`/research` is the owner's symlink to `/mnt/data2tb/research-cloud`; use the real path until it exists). Keep an uncompressed final copy with the ZIP's folder structure. Upload through `/research/.tools/research_cache.py upload PR-<n>-<name> <zip>`; `receipt` verifies files already on Drive and `status` lists folders. It uses immutable one-way `rclone copyto`, compares Drive MD5/size and local ZIP-member size/CRC32, and writes `.drive-receipt.json`. Record Drive ID, MD5, SHA256, manifest/readback and both cache and `rclone copy gdrive:...` retrieval paths in RESULTS_INDEX.

On M1/M4, `~/Local/Research-Cloud` is READ-ONLY: fetch missing data, copy it out to an ignored working directory, never write/delete inside it or force offloading. Prefer restoring Drive files through bb128 to fill the cache; the often-away M1 may keep fetching through Drive. Live training, audits, caches and restored inputs stay on NVMe. Keep current methods for already running PR301, PR306 and other open campaigns until they finish. Do not rely on the planned private bb128↔M4 Ethernet link (192.168.77.1/192.168.77.2, no internet route) until confirmed working.

The owner explicitly authorized the existing bb128 user cron at 04:00 daily to evict only this HDD cache: seven days without research-file reads/writes, complete unchanged receipt coverage, current matching Drive size/MD5, final local content reread with O_NOATIME, no open owning PR, no KEEP, and no open process. Receipt/folder-listing activity does not count as use. If GitHub's open-PR list is unavailable, nothing is evicted or purged. Evicted folders move to `/research/.evicted/`; purge waits another seven days from the eviction name stamp, not the directory mtime. `/research/.eviction-log.jsonl` records decisions, available Drive IDs and restore commands. This exception does not apply to NVMe/Mac originals or general cleanup. Repository deletion-logic fixes need owner review and deployment approval; do not modify the live tool, cron or cache contents during this import/testing task.

Only one large upload at a time on the shared uplink, without competition with Slumbot. Never use `sync`, `move`, `delete`, `purge`, `dedupe`, `--delete-*` or overwrite/remove existing Drive files against `gdrive:`. Never read/print/copy/archive/commit/transmit `~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf` or other credentials; agents never create/change Google credentials or sharing. Report disk/auth/network/symlink blockers in the PR and wait instead of presenting incomplete work as finished. See [docs/artifact-storage.md](docs/artifact-storage.md) and the [copied deployed README](docs/research-cache-deployed-readme.md).

Agents retrieve models into ignored local working directories (`models/`, `results/` or `planning/`) when needed and verify their SHA256 against the index/manifest. Record the Drive URL or ID, archive member path, hash and retrieval command in RESULTS_INDEX.md. Do not force-add research binaries. A model may enter Git only as an explicitly owner-approved release artifact with an exact-path ignore exception; a release name alone does not grant approval. Existing released models remain available through their pinned release downloads.

For the shared Git/Drive boundary and required archive receipts, follow [docs/artifact-storage.md](docs/artifact-storage.md). Before committing, stage the intended files and run `python -m scripts.check_repository_artifacts`; CI rejects new research payloads and changes to grandfathered payload bytes. Do not regenerate the retention list to admit new experiment output. [docs/development.md](docs/development.md) maps supported runtime and research entry points.
