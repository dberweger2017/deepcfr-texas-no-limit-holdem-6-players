# Repository guidance

Read [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) before starting a task. Its **How we work** section defines the branch, commit, push, PR, review, and merge workflow, along with code-comment conventions and decisions reserved for the owner.

Use **Current position** to select the next task and update it when work lands. The roadmap records direction and acceptance checks; it does not schedule unattended work. Consult [docs/rules.md](docs/rules.md) before changing game behavior or player information.

Large untracked artifacts and cleanup destinations are indexed in [RESULTS_INDEX.md](RESULTS_INDEX.md). Keep working files wherever convenient; at cleanup archive them to the designated Google Drive folder with manifests, failures and retrieval provenance. The owner authorized removing local research originals once Google Drive confirms their matching archives uploaded. Apply that authorization only to merged PR evidence after current upload acceptance and dependency review. For cleanup, trust Google Drive's confirmed upload status and use existing manifests and provenance; the owner does not require downloading archives or repeating archive/member hash audits. Check that selected originals still match their recorded paths, sizes and modification history; retain changed or uncertain files. Retain open PR roots, active inputs, shared Git, changed or unarchived files and other agents’ dependencies. Never delete inside synced folders or force offloading. Record removed paths and restoration links in a cleanup receipt and the index. This policy does not schedule unattended cleanup.

Before copying, archiving, moving or cleaning research files, check the owning PR’s current status. Leave open PR work roots and their dependencies untouched unless the owner explicitly authorizes that specific action. Finish archival work on merged runs without interfering with active research.

The owner gave standing permission on October 14 to remove inactive, rebuildable caches and other regenerable output without separate approval when there is no data loss and no current active PR is affected. This includes derived policy caches and build output even when excluded from research archives. Confirm their generating inputs and rebuild method, protect active dependencies, and record removed paths plus source/input pins and regeneration commands. Unique scientific evidence, checkpoints and inference originals still follow the upload/retention rules above; being repeatable does not turn a research run into a cache. This permission does not schedule unattended cleanup.

Research model storage: training checkpoints, inference exports, replay buffers and generated research bundles belong in the designated Google Drive research folder. On M1/M4 use `~/Local/Research-Cloud/PR-<n>-<slug>/` through native Drive desktop. On cygnus-x1 (`ssh bb128`) the standard is `/mnt/data2tb/research-cloud/PR-<n>-<slug>/` on the HDD, uploaded directly with `rclone copy --immutable --transfers 1` to `gdrive:PR-<n>-<slug>/`; use the exact same existing per-PR name. The remote is rooted at Research-Cloud, ID `188bEt6i0RHqegCCdvpf3wPzUiRw78N2s`; no Mac staging is needed.

Confirm the HDD is mounted and `rclone lsd gdrive:` works before use. Live training, audits, caches and restored inputs stay on NVMe. Require zero-difference `rclone check --one-way` using Drive MD5, record its output/exit status and `rclone lsjson` file IDs plus local archive/manifest SHA256s; retain HDD copies as restore caches. Remove an HDD copy only after accepted upload, no active dependency and a cleanup receipt; NVMe originals retain the existing merged-PR rules.

Only one large upload at a time on the shared uplink, without competition with Slumbot. Never use `sync`, `move`, `delete`, `purge`, `dedupe`, `--delete-*` or overwrite/remove existing Drive files. Never print/copy/archive/commit/transmit `~/.config/rclone/rclone.conf` or other credentials; agents never create/change Google credentials or sharing. Report disk/auth/network blockers in the PR and leave acceptance pending.

Agents retrieve models into ignored local working directories (`models/`, `results/` or `planning/`) when needed and verify their SHA256 against the index/manifest. Record the Drive URL or ID, archive member path, hash and retrieval command in RESULTS_INDEX.md. Do not force-add research binaries. A model may enter Git only as an explicitly owner-approved release artifact with an exact-path ignore exception; a release name alone does not grant approval. Existing released models remain available through their pinned release downloads.

For the shared Git/Drive boundary and required archive receipts, follow [docs/artifact-storage.md](docs/artifact-storage.md). Before committing, stage the intended files and run `python -m scripts.check_repository_artifacts`; CI rejects new research payloads and changes to grandfathered payload bytes. Do not regenerate the retention list to admit new experiment output. [docs/development.md](docs/development.md) maps supported runtime and research entry points.
