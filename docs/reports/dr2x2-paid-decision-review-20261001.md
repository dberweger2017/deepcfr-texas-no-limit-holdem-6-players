## Two interpretation corrections

Compression changes earlier-street information entering flop keys; it does not
literally leave every flop key unchanged. Its measured flop partition/coverage
change is small. Most direct coverage gain is late, while earlier regret updates
can still respond to better late continuation values.

The#143 uncapped-first-bet LBR screen records v1 mean fold probability51.4%
against a36.9%sizing screen (227decisions). For a bet b into pot P, b/(P+b) is
an idealized zero-equity-bluff break-even fold/MDF benchmark, not a pot-odds
upper bound on permissible folding. Selected reached holdings do not establish
range-wide overfolding or the EV of an individual call. Early betting-stop
whole-hand losses likewise do not locate the erroneous street/action.
