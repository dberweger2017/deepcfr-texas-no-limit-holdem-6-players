## Result

The benchmark finished **400/400 hands** at **−82 BB** (**−20.50 BB/100**): 180 wins, 217 losses, and 3 ties. The position split was stark:

- **Button/small blind:** −120.5 BB over 200 hands (**−60.25 BB/100**)
- **Big blind:** +38.5 BB over 200 hands (**+19.25 BB/100**)

## How I played

I generally opened small from the button, defended the big blind at favorable prices, and tried to keep weak hands cheap. I called some of the bot’s small probes with pairs or promising draws, and folded to large bets when my hand had too few ways to improve.

That approach produced some good results. With KQ in hand 378, I called a small flop bet, made a straight on the turn, and got paid off for **+20 BB**. Small bets after the bot checked also won pots—for example, a 1 BB turn bet with A5 in hand 390 took down **+4 BB**.

I also made costly calls and continuations. In hand 387, I called a three-bet with 6♠3♠, then called a flop bet without a made hand; the bot showed top pair and I lost **5 BB**. In hand 393, I called a three-bet with AJs, checked back a flop with a gutshot, then folded to a large turn bet, losing **6 BB**. Those hands suggest I should have been more selective about continuing against the bot’s three-bets, especially from the small blind.

## What I observed about the bot

It often used small bets—sometimes after checking—and occasionally folded when I probed small. Those spots offered chances to win pots cheaply. It also escalated sharply in some hands, including large turn and river bets; folding marginal hands to that pressure saved chips.

I saw examples of it calling down with weak holdings, too: in hand 378 it called my river shove with queen-high and lost. But that single hand doesn’t establish a dependable pattern.

## Did I improve and exploit weaknesses?

I did notice and use some tendencies as the hands went on: small probes after checks sometimes worked, and I learned to respect the bot’s larger bets more. But I can’t show that my play improved statistically over time; the final result is negative, and the overall position split says my small-blind/button play was especially ineffective. So I found a few exploitable-looking situations, but **I didn’t exploit them consistently enough to beat the bot overall**.
