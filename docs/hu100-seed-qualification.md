## Fresh paired evaluation and frozen interpretation

Evaluate all three lineages anew, on the same fresh paired schedule. For each
lineage run early/off, terminal/off and terminal/on against random, check_call,
tight_aggressive, loose_aggressive and pot_pressure. Every independent block
contains both swapped seats; candidate and rival private action streams are
paired across policies and lineages. One uniform reference is played once and
reused exactly. Policies see entitled observations only, never evaluator seeds,
decks, rival hole cards or future outcomes.

Primary growth family: terminal/off minus early/off for tight_aggressive and
loose_aggressive separately for each seed (six fixed contrasts). Use paired
block means and two-sided Student-t **99.1666667% intervals**, Bonferroni
familywise alpha .05 across all six, regardless of incomplete panels.

Separate translation family: terminal/on minus terminal/off against
pot_pressure separately for each seed (three fixed contrasts). Use paired
block means and two-sided Student-t **98.3333333% intervals**, Bonferroni
familywise alpha .05 across the three. These are two separately labeled
families; do not claim .05 control across their union. Also show unadjusted
95% intervals as descriptive deal uncertainty.

For each contrast: lower >0 means detectable improvement, upper <0 means
decline, otherwise inconclusive. A practically supported improvement requires
the adjusted lower bound >**10 BB/100**, fixed here before outcomes. Report
each opponent's growth and the translation effect as repeating only if its
contrast improves in all three completed seeds; say separately whether all
three clear the practical threshold. Overall recipe qualification requires
all six growth and all three translation contrasts improve, all three fixed
1B endpoints complete, all integrity/replay/reproduction/storage/evidence gates
pass, exact on-menu controls match and no random severe-regression flag occurs.
The random flag is a conservative operational safeguard, not an extra formal
statistical claim or proof of noninferiority when absent. Inconclusive
tight growth cannot be silently replaced by loose-only success.

Keep check_call/tight_aggressive/loose_aggressive as exact on-menu translation
controls: complete actions, events and settlements must match on/off for each
terminal policy. Any mismatch blocks acceptance. Random translation is a
descriptive off-menu control with a predeclared severe-regression flag if its
ordinary paired 95% upper bound is below -20 BB/100; it is not a formal
noninferiority claim. Growth on random/check_call/pot_pressure is descriptive.
Translation uses #205's fixed limits (512 states, 128 events) and one unchanged
private draw. Report latency, exact/translated/uniform rates, distances, search
work, coverage and visits by street as descriptive, policy-dependent quantities.

Show the three effect estimates, range and sample standard deviation as
