# Proposed full-game K50 confirmation

**Proposal for the owner's go, not an authorized run.** PR235 makes full-game evaluation possible after #232's trained-bench pass. It does not adopt K50, change a release/default, or start a campaign. The rising held-out bench loss (0.96 at 1M to 1.04 at 10M) makes a full learning curve necessary. Run only when the owner releases the M4 from its current HU100 campaign and approves the quote and frozen protocol.

Three HU20 K50 lineages: training seeds **2026100601, 2026100602, 2026100603**, linear CFR, opponent-sampled averaging, uniform zero-mass/missing fallback, unchanged ordered history and native legal menu. Pin all three #163 tables, source, native binary, every checkpoint/export and engine. No regret floor, search, translation, K200, alternate fallback or seed selection. Proposed complete-iteration checkpoint ladder: **140M, 700M, 1.4B, 2.8B, 7B, 14B, 28B actual nodes**, with **28B per seed as the fixed maximum** (84B total). Each checkpoint is audited independently against its average and current exports. Reproduce exact recovery on a small prefix before the main budget; defects stop dependent work.

The 1.4× scaling is an initial budget estimate, not evidence of matched density. Compare against the indexed v1 opponent-sampled 100M/500M/1B/2B/5B/10B lineage checkpoints where available. Report actual nodes/iterations/keys, total and positive visits per key, zero-visit fraction, and per-street minimum/p10/p25/median/p75/p90/p99/maximum for each checkpoint. At 14B versus v1 10B, require mean traverser visits/key within **±5% per seed**, and disclose distribution/per-street mismatches. A miss is reported as unmatched; do not silently use a different checkpoint or extend the budget. The 28B endpoint supplies a second, fixed plateau comparison rather than another matched-density claim.

Use **8,192 duplicate blocks per seed/checkpoint** for descriptive direct learning curves against the same pinned v0.4.2, on a separate learning root. Also compare 7B→14B and 14B→28B with 8,192 blocks per seed/contrast. Operational plateau means both successive gain intervals have upper bounds **<+1 BB/100**; a wide interval or a larger gain means plateau is unestablished at the maximum. This is a limited full-game performance plateau, not CFR convergence. Do not select the best observed checkpoint: **28B is the terminal primary for every seed**, regardless of curve shape. If plateau/density checks fail, describe that limitation and ask the owner about a new protocol; no automatic extension or replacement test.

## Provisional M4 quote and admission

**Sequential evaluation, one research worker at a time** is the proposed safe starting point. Each K50 process has **1,384,031,032 table bytes (1.29 GiB, about 1.4 GB)**, memory-mapped in Python; native training currently reads the same tables into its process. K50 adds about 1.4× v1's keys. Memory sharing does not reduce the conservative sum-of-RSS guard; never assume three workers fit simply because mappings share physical pages.

The #185 M4 v1 10B training time was 63–65 minutes. Scaling node work and the measured M1 v1/K50 speed ratio gives approximately **3.6–4.1 hours per 28B K50 seed**, **11–13 hours training**, or **14–16 hours with 25% headroom**. This is extrapolation, not a new M4 measurement. Plan training peak **3.5–4.5 GiB** including the tables; sequential candidate+release compact inference/LBR peak **3–5 GiB**. Entry growth, transient export/load buffers and LBR caches must be measured before admission.

#188 completed about 295k LBR hands in 7.8 hours with three v1 workers. Conservatively serialize and allow K50 lookup overhead: **18–25 hours** for the proposed 197k LBR hands, plus **3–5 hours** direct/pressure/scripted/learning play and **2–4 hours** audits/export/reporting. Provisional all-work quote **40–50 M4 hours**, including headroom. Recommend a **50-hour aggregate ceiling**, subject to a fresh cost-only pilot; no paid compute. Reserve **60 GiB free disk at entry** and preserve a **16 GiB floor**. A smaller quote must come from measured cost, never from weakened gates.

Before main training/evaluation, independently admit a maximum **90-minute cost-only pilot**, excluded from science: checkpoint/table loading, short native training/recovery, 1,024 LBR blocks/target, 2,048 direct blocks/seed, 256 pressure and 32 other-panel blocks/target. Inspect only timing, memory, completeness and paired SDs. Verify projected final widths at the fixed counts above. If any width misses its target or total quote exceeds 50 hours, stop and bring a revised concrete proposal to the owner before final outcomes; do not automatically resize or launch. K50's visit/plateau curve is disclosed even if direct play wins.

Whole-family RSS **<7 GiB**, normal pressure, ongoing free memory **≥15%**, entry headroom **≥8 GiB**, fixed-baseline swap growth **≤512 MiB**, total swap **≤2 GiB**, AC throughout, disk floor and per-phase/aggregate deadlines are mandatory. Enforce continuously over supervisors, workers, exports, audits and archiving; stop only owned processes. A sensor failure, information leak, invalid action, replay/accounting error, nonfinite result, hash mismatch or resource violation stops the campaign and preserves partials/failures. No failed-hand reruns. If the existing M4 baseline cannot meet these guards, wait for explicit owner admission rather than changing them.

This proposal authorizes no model publication. Even a pass only supplies evidence for a separate owner decision on K50 adoption and any release.
